Moca Technology became the official member of the Mobile Marketing Association (MMA) APAC region recently.

MMA is one of the most authoritative associations in mobile marketing industries, aiming at shaping the successful future for marketers by enlightening, empowering, and enabling them. MMA has members across the different regions that have outstanding performance in the industries. One of the most influential parts that MMA hosted is the SMARTIES Award, which is the goal for marketers to chase every year.

As being a member of MMA, MOCA will keep following the mission to provide the innovated and customized marketing solutions for clients to propel their business growth.
