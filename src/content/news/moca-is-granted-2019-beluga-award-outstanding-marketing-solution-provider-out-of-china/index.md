MOCA is thrilled to be awarded the “2019 Beluga Award Outstanding Marketing Solution Provider Out of China” in the Global Traffic Conference summit on October 25th, 2019. It is the first time that the GTC committee has granted the Beluga Award to those Chinese companies who did a marvelous job in the market out of China.

As the winner, MOCA has been dedicating to serve both Chinese and oversea advertisers for many years. The award committee said, “By contributing to the mobile advertising industry for seven years, MOCA started its business in India and extended the market to Indonesia, Bangladesh, Vietnam, Myanmar, Pakistan, etc. As being the exclusive partner with Xiaomi, Cheetah Mobile, Meitu, Zapya, etc., MOCA is the pioneer of Chinese companies to impart the developed technique, experience, and service overseas and unearths the market opportunities for brands to enhance the communication with their users.”
