The mobile app industry is set to experience explosive growth in 2025, and gaming is at the forefront of this expansion. With over 6 billion smartphone users globally, the mobile app economy is forecasted to surpass a $626 billion market size by 2030, according to 2025 Mobile App Trends released by Adjust. Mobile ad spending is also on the rise, with the U.S. alone expected to reach $228.11 billion in 2025, capturing a significant share of digital ad budgets.

**Gaming’s Continued Dominance**

Mobile gaming remains a major driver in the app ecosystem, with global revenue projected to reach $126.1 billion in 2025. Games were the most popular app category on both the App Store and Google Play in 2024. Asia continues to dominate, with 13 out of the top 15 highest-grossing mobile games based in the region. Mobile games accounted for 49% of global gaming revenue, outpacing console (28%) and PC (23%) gaming. With the global number of mobile gamers set to grow by 8%, reaching 1.9 billion by 2025, trends like ultra-casual games, IP-based games, and AI-driven hyperpersonalization are expected to fuel further expansion, particularly in high-growth regions like India and Southeast Asia.

### **Key Trends for Gaming Advertisers in 2025**

### 1. AI-Powered Precision: Beyond Generic Targeting

AI and machine learning are no longer optional. By 2025, predictive analytics and hyperpersonalized campaigns will dominate user acquisition, enabling advertisers to identify high-intent gamers and optimize ROI. Tools like incrementality testing and behavior-based targeting will separate winners from the pack. Savvy brands are already partnering with experts fluent in AI-driven frameworks to future-proof their strategies.

### 2. Privacy Compliance as a Competitive Edge

With stricter data regulations, privacy-first marketing is nonnegotiable. Solutions like SKAN and Privacy Sandbox will balance compliance with actionable insights. Success lies in adopting measurement tools that respect user trust while delivering campaign clarity. Forward-thinking teams are aligning with partners skilled in navigating this nuanced terrain.

### **3****.** Seamless Multi-Platform Storytelling

Gamers now toggle effortlessly between apps, CTV, and social platforms. By integrating in-app ads with CTV campaigns (forecasted at $34.3B in 2025), advertisers can craft cohesive narratives that amplify engagement. Brands excelling here are those blending creativity with cross-channel analytics to sustain player immersion.

### 4\. Hyper-Casual Dominance & Emerging Markets

Ultra-casual games and IP-based titles are fueling growth in India, Southeast Asia, and beyond. With mobile gaming revenue hitting $126.1B in 2025, localized, culturally resonant ads will drive installs. Success stories often involve collaboration with regional influencers and data-savvy creators to tap into high-growth audiences.

5**.** **Retention: The New Acquisition Frontier**

Rising user costs make retention critical. AI-driven re-engagement campaigns and dynamic ad experiences will boost lifetime value. Leaders in this space leverage predictive modeling to keep players hooked—turning casual users into loyal advocates.

### Staying Ahead: A Blueprint for 2025

-   Diversify channels: Blend in-app ads, CTV, and social media to meet gamers everywhere.
-   Embed AI: Automate decisions, from budget allocation to creative optimization.
-   Prioritize privacy: Build trust while unlocking insights.
-   Think retention-first: Personalize experiences to maximize LTV.
